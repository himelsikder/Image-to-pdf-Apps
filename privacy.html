🔒 Summary: Img to Pdf Offline is a 100% offline, local-processing utility app. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any user data, images, or documents to any remote server or third party.

1. Introduction & Principle of Zero-Knowledge

This Privacy Policy governs the mobile and web application Img to Pdf Offline (Package: com.imgtopdf.offline), developed by Himel Sikder.

We believe that your personal photos, official documents, certificates, and invoices are strictly your own property. The application is built with a strict "privacy-by-design" architecture: it functions completely offline with zero internet access required to convert or manage documents.

2. Local On-Device Processing (Zero Server Transmission)

All core functionalities operate strictly within your device's local memory (RAM) and sandbox storage:

  • Converting images (JPG, PNG, WebP) to PDF
  • Extracting pages from PDF files as images
  • Compressing and resizing images
  • Merging multiple PDF files into one
  • Splitting, rotating, or rearranging PDF pages
  • Adding digital signatures, passwords, and watermarks

No files or images are ever uploaded to any cloud server or database.

3. Device Permissions & Justification (Google Play Policy)

The app requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for user-initiated actions:

  • Photos and Media / Storage (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Storage): Requested solely when you manually choose files from your gallery to convert.
  • Camera (Optional): Requested only if you tap the "Camera Snap" feature to take a photo of a document. Images are processed locally and never transmitted.
  • Downloads / File Storage: Used exclusively to save the converted PDF documents or images into your device's local storage.

4. Third-Party Services, Ads, and Analytics

  • No Advertising SDKs: The app contains zero ad networks (No Google AdMob, Unity, AppLovin, etc.).
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: We do not include any tracking software, telemetry SDKs, or device fingerprinting.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any email address or personal credentials to use the application.

5. Google Play Console Data Safety Compliance

  • Data Collected: None (0 bytes).
  • Data Shared with Third Parties: None.
  • Ephemeral Processing: Yes. Files only reside in volatile device memory during the active session.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: Users have complete control. You can clear temporary cache at any time using the "Clear Cache" button in the app settings.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Img to Pdf Offline does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, our application complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies.
